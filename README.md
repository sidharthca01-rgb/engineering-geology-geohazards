# Engineering Geology and Geohazards

> Geological characterization and introductory assessment workflows for engineering conditions and geohazards.

## About

This repository organizes geological information relevant to engineering projects and natural hazards. Possible content includes rock-mass descriptions, discontinuity surveys, weathering grades, slope observations, landslide inventories, and susceptibility-mapping exercises.

All assessments must state their scale, assumptions, data quality, and limitations. A portfolio exercise is not a design certification, site clearance, or substitute for qualified engineering and geotechnical review.

## Scope

- Engineering geological site descriptions
- Rock-mass and discontinuity observations
- Weathering and material characterization
- Slope processes and landslide inventories
- Geohazard mapping concepts
- Risk communication and uncertainty

## Data and scientific integrity

- Identify the source, author/organization, date, licence, and version of external data.
- Record coordinate reference systems, units, scale, and methods where relevant.
- Do not invent field observations, analytical results, coordinates, or citations.
- Label synthetic/demo data clearly; never present it as field or laboratory data.
- Include uncertainty, assumptions, and limitations in interpretations.
- Respect site access, safety, privacy, and data-sharing restrictions.
